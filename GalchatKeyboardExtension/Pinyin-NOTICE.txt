The Swift lookup implementation is part of Jarvis. No Rime executable engine
or third-party runtime is included. Input is processed locally. This keyboard
supports full Pinyin with v for ü and apostrophes for syllable boundaries.
It does not implement fuzzy Pinyin, abbreviation, personal learning, voice
input, or Apple's predictive keyboard services.
